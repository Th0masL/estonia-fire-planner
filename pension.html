What a salary is worth as a pension

Estonian pension maths is counter-intuitive in two ways that matter when deciding whether a job is worth taking. The state adds 4% of gross to your second pillar on top of your own contribution — money you get only if you are employed. And the first €8,400 a year of any salary is free of income tax, so a modest wage keeps far more of itself than a large one.

Health cover

Estonian health cover follows social tax, not residency. Someone not employed generally needs a voluntary contract, so a job removes that cost. Untick if cover comes from somewhere else — parental benefit, raising a child under 3, or a large family.
