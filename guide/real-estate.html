Property and rental income

Estonia treats residential rental income unusually kindly, which makes property a more serious option here than in most of Europe.

  • Rental income (individual): 22% on 80% of gross → 17.6% effective. Very favourable, and no depreciation bookkeeping required
  • Rental income (via OÜ): full expense deduction incl. interest and depreciation, 0% until distribution. Better at scale or when leveraged
  • Own home sale: exempt when the property was your actual residence up to the sale (TuMS §15(5)(1)) ✅ — but only one such sale every two years (§15(6)), counted per person and running from Land Register registration, not the contract date. A second home sale inside that window is fully taxable at 22%
  • Land tax: rising after the 2024+ reform — check the specific plot, increases have been capped but are compounding
  • Mortgage interest: the personal deduction was abolished; only deductible through a company

The strategic question isn't "is Tallinn property a good investment" — it's correlation. Estonian salary + Estonian property + Estonian bank + Estonian pension is one bet, repeated four times. See risks.md.
