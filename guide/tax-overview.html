Estonian tax in one screen

Item Rate Notes
Personal income tax 22% flat ✅ Raised from 20% on 2025-01-01. The planned 24% "security tax" was enacted in 2024 then repealed in 2025 — it never took effect
Basic exemption €700/month (€8,400/yr) ✅ From 2026-01-01, universal — the income-based taper ("maksuküür") abolished. €776/mo at pension age
Additional exemption for children None ✅ §23¹ repealed from 2024, not 2026. Partly replaced by a ~€100/yr per child motor-vehicle tax reduction
Capital gains 22% No separate CGT regime — gains are ordinary income
Dividends from EE companies 0% to the individual Already taxed at company level
Foreign dividends 0% if taxed abroad ✅ TuMS §18(1¹): exempt if corporate tax was paid on the profit or tax was withheld on the dividend. Otherwise 22%. Must still be declared (Table 8.8)
Residential rental income 17.6% effective ✅ 22% on 80% of gross — automatic 20% deduction, no receipts needed. Residential only
Corporate tax on retained earnings 0% The headline feature
Corporate tax on distributions 22/78 = 22% of the gross amount
Social tax 33% On salary/board fees. Not on dividends. Minimum base €886/mo → €292.38/mo
Wealth tax None
Inheritance / gift tax None
VAT 24% Raised 2025-07-01
Declaration deadline 30 April For the previous calendar year
Payment / refund deadline 1 October Anyone declaring foreign income or capital gains settles on this later track, not the March refund wave

The shape of this: Estonia taxes labour moderately and capital lightly, with no wealth or inheritance tax. That is close to an ideal jurisdiction for accumulating and holding a FIRE portfolio. The friction is concentrated in one place — health insurance.
