# Buying a home

A near-term home purchase can constrain the rest of a FIRE plan, because the deposit must remain liquid while the future mortgage and running costs alter long-term spending.

> **Limited review, 20 September 2026:** regulatory limits and the named tax/provider sources were checked. Payment examples are conditional arithmetic, not offers or forecasts. Personal eligibility and contract-specific questions remain open.

## Protect money needed for a dated purchase

A purchase reserve should not depend on selling equities at a favourable price.
A market loss near completion can force a delay or leave a funding shortfall.
There is no precise three-year boundary that makes equity investment safe.

The [portfolio plan](portfolio.md) applies to surplus beyond the full purchase
budget and emergency reserve, not automatically to the down payment.

## Where the down payment should sit

Choose by the payment deadline, loss tolerance and access requirements—not a
universal product ranking.

| Option | Key condition |
|---|---|
| Eligible instant-access deposit | Confirm guarantee coverage, withdrawal limits and transfer timing |
| Term deposit | Match maturity to the purchase; check early-exit restrictions |
| Money market fund or overnight-rate ETF | Accept investment risk and confirm redemption/trading, settlement and onward transfer |
| Equity fund | Do not rely on selling at a favourable price to meet a fixed near-term payment |

**Tax deferral is not tax-free interest.** A house payment can use up the remaining
investment-account contribution allowance; excess withdrawals can be taxable.
The [sourced cash example](brokers.md#tax-deferral-is-not-a-tax-exemption) shows why
contributing €50,000 and withdrawing €51,000 does not make the €1,000 gain exempt.
Qualifying deposits can also use investment-account treatment under the relevant
conditions; funds do not have an exclusive tax advantage.

Confirm the actual payment timetable and keep the emergency reserve separate.
A fund is not deposit-guaranteed, and settlement does not guarantee cash will
reach the seller on a particular day. See
[product risks and access](brokers.md#deposits-and-funds-do-different-jobs).

## Estonian mortgage mechanics

[Eesti Pank's housing-loan requirements](https://www.eestipank.ee/en/financial-stability/requirements-housing-loans),
checked 20 September 2026:

| Limit | General requirement |
|---|---|
| Loan-to-value (loan / collateral value) | 85%; up to 90% with a state guarantee |
| Debt service / net income | 50%, counting all loan principal and interest payments |
| Maximum maturity | 30 years |
| Variable-rate affordability calculation | Higher of the contract rate and 6% |

Banks can exceed the limits for up to 15% of quarterly housing-loan volume.
That flexibility is not an applicant's entitlement, and meeting a limit is not
loan approval or proof that the payments suit your FIRE plan.

The rules are **not unchanged since 2015**. Eesti Pank
[changed the stress-rate calculation from 1 April 2024](https://www.eestipank.ee/en/press/eesti-pank-amending-requirements-banks-regulating-issuing-new-housing-loans-26012024):
the former contract-rate-plus-two-percentage-points component was removed.
Run your own higher-rate scenarios as well.

### Costs and eligibility to confirm

Ask for a written offer identifying the margin, reference rate, reset dates,
any floor or fixed-rate period, repayment schedule and fees. Do not assume every
loan uses six-month Euribor or that today's quote applies for the full term.

Budget separately for the valuation, notary, state and bank fees, moving,
furnishing, repairs, insurance and property-specific land tax. Use actual quotes,
not a universal percentage of the purchase price. Keep the emergency reserve
outside the deposit budget.

**Still unverified:** current EIS/KredEx guarantee categories, individual eligibility,
guarantee charges and restrictions, lender age limits, insurance requirements,
local land tax and any relationship discount. Confirm these with the relevant
provider before relying on them. The previous categorical eligibility claims
and estimated margin range are not retained as verified guidance.

The personal housing-loan interest deduction ended from 2024, according to
[EMTA](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/housing-loan-interest).
This does not make the quoted interest rate an all-in cost: fees still matter.

## Loan term, deposit size, and the bank relationship

### Loan term: compare cash flow and total cost

A longer term lowers the required payment but increases total interest at the
same positive rate. Whether investing the payment difference produces greater
wealth depends on realised returns after costs and taxes, timing and risk.
It does **not** establish an unchanged FI date or a universally best term.

Illustrative €300,000 loan, constant 4% nominal annual rate, monthly annuity
payments, no fees or overpayments; figures rounded only after calculation:

| Term | Monthly payment | Total interest |
|---|---:|---:|
| 15 years | €2,219 | €99,431 |
| 20 years | €1,818 | €136,306 |
| 25 years | €1,584 | €175,053 |
| 30 years | €1,432 | €215,609 |

These are payment illustrations, not FI simulations. Compare plans with the same
household budget, investment assumptions and end date, including remaining debt.
The planner models dated mortgage payments; it does not require every household
to clear its mortgage on the FI date. Check a proposed term against the lender's
offer and retirement-income assessment.

### Deposit size: compare actual offers, not a universal optimum

Preserve purchase costs and emergency cash first. Ask banks to quote several
feasible deposit sizes. A larger deposit reduces borrowing, but it also reduces
liquid assets. Any margin improvement must come from an actual offer.

**Hypothetical starting-balance comparison:** on a €360,000 property, suppose
a €270,000 loan costs 3.70% and a €306,000 loan costs 4.00%.
Starting annualised interest is €9,990 versus €12,240: a €2,250 difference for
€36,000 more deposit, or 6.25%. This is not actual first-year amortising interest
or a guaranteed annual return. It assumes the quoted margin changes for the
whole loan; real balances and floating rates change.

There is no verified rule to deposit exactly the amount at a particular LTV band.
Compare affordability, liquidity and total cost, not just the initial margin.

### Does holding your ETF at LHV get you a better rate?

**Not verified.** Ask each lender whether account use or bundled products change
the offer and get the conditions in writing. Compare total bundle costs,
including investment custody and transfer charges. Do not move investments
based on an assumed mortgage discount.

## Funding the down payment from crypto

If any of it comes from crypto, establish the treatment of the actual holdings
before budgeting. Qualifying MiCA acquisitions and investment-account holdings
are not interchangeable with non-qualifying crypto. See the sourced
[crypto guidance](crypto.md) for the loss-offset and reporting distinctions.

**Practical implications:**

1. **Compute net proceeds before committing.** For a single taxable sale outside an investment account, €60,000 proceeds minus €20,000 basis gives €40,000 gain. At an assumed 22% rate, with no fees, deductible losses or other relief, reserve €8,800 and budget €51,200. This is a conditional illustration, not the tax on every crypto sale.
2. **Keep the tax reserve separate.** Check the applicable declaration and payment deadlines with EMTA; this example does not establish them. The simulator reserves estimated tax immediately, not on the statutory payment date.
3. **Check transaction history and timing.** Do not assume splitting sales has no effect on the final bill, or that wallet location determines eligibility. The simulator's aggregate crypto estimate is not a transaction-level calculation.

## What this does to the FIRE plan

Model the new running costs, not just the mortgage. As a simple withdrawal-rate
illustration, an extra €400 per month is €4,800 annually; dividing by an assumed
3.5% gives approximately €137,143 of additional portfolio target. That is not
the simulator's full calculation or a guarantee that costs persist unchanged.
Mortgage payments have an end date; maintenance, insurance and utilities need
their own assumptions.

**Residence-sale relief is conditional.** [EMTA's residence guidance](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/taxable-income/transfer-immovable-property/transfer-place-residence)
describes an exemption for a dwelling used as a residence up to sale, generally
usable for one residence sale within two years. Check actual use and any mixed
business use; do not assume every home or all home equity is automatically tax-free.

## When does prepaying beat investing?

Prepayment reduces debt and future interest, but money used for it is no longer
liquid. Compare the interest avoided, repayment fees, any tax cost of obtaining
the cash and the return you might earn elsewhere **after costs and taxes**.

For floating-rate debt, future interest savings change with the rate. An expected
equity return is uncertain and is not directly equivalent to avoided borrowing
costs. The previous fixed rate thresholds and “invest everything” recommendations
were not established as suitable for every household.

Investment-account contributions are not a permanent exemption for investment
profits. Withdrawals use the remaining allowance; see
[the investment-account guide](investment-account.md).
Do not compare a guaranteed mortgage saving with an assumed tax-free 7% equity
return.

### What a rate rise actually costs you

Illustrative €300,000, 30-year monthly annuity loan; each row holds its rate
constant for the whole term, excludes fees and rounds at the end:

| Nominal annual rate | Monthly payment | Total interest |
|---|---:|---:|
| 4% | €1,432 | €215,609 |
| 5% | €1,610 | €279,767 |
| 6% | €1,799 | €347,515 |
| 7% | €1,996 | €418,527 |

These are separate scenarios, not a forecast of Euribor resets. A later rate
change uses the balance and remaining term then applicable.

### Practical mechanics

**Provider snapshot, 20 September 2026:** [LHV's home-loan FAQ](https://www.lhv.ee/en/faq/home-loan)
advertises free early repayment, including partial repayment.
[Swedbank's home-loan fees](https://www.swedbank.ee/private/credit/loans/home?language=ENG),
effective 31 August 2026, list early repayment according to the agreement and
a one-month-interest charge in digital channels, excluding fixed-rate loans
and technical limitations. These are not interchangeable tariffs or a quotation
for an existing contract.

Before paying extra, request a written calculation and revised schedule:

- What fee applies to this amount, date and interest-rate arrangement?
- Does advance notice change it? What notice and processing time are required?
- Can you reduce the term, payment, or both? Are amendments chargeable?
- How much interest is avoided under the same rate assumptions?
- How much accessible emergency cash remains?

**Still unverified:** the applicable statutory compensation provisions, fixed-rate
break costs, notice waivers and schedule choices for your agreement. The former
universal three-month cap, fixed-rate percentage caps and “every bank” assertions
have been removed rather than presented as legal advice.

Keeping a higher payment can repay principal faster; lowering the required
payment improves monthly flexibility. Compare written schedules on the same
cash-flow basis. Accessing prepaid equity later may require new borrowing and
approval; do not count it as emergency cash. Neither a compulsory payoff near
FIRE nor investing instead is universally best.

## Sequencing — what to do in what order

1. Seek an indicative affordability assessment, then confirm the property-specific offer and conditions.
2. Reserve the down payment, quoted transaction costs, moving/furnishing, repairs and any tax due, plus a separate emergency fund.
3. Choose where to hold the purchase reserve using the risk and access checks above.
4. Invest only money demonstrably surplus to those reserves.
5. Before signing, reconcile the payment timetable, financing conditions and cash availability.
6. After purchase, update the FIRE plan with actual housing costs and review it when rates or household circumstances change.

A small ETF standing order can test account mechanics, but it must not put a
dated purchase or emergency reserve at market risk.
