# Estonian tax in one screen

**Scope:** selected rules for an Estonian tax-resident individual in **2026**,
with company rules identified separately. Limited source review: **20 September
2026**. This is not a complete tax return or a personal tax ruling; future rules
and cross-border outcomes are not guaranteed.

| Item | Rate / treatment | Important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Personal income tax | **{{incomeTax|pct}}** standard rate | Applies to taxable income, not every receipt. Exemptions, deductions and special pension rates can change the result |
| Basic exemption | **{{basicExemptionMonthly|money}}/month ({{basicExemptionMonthly|money12}}/year)** | From 1 January 2026, no income taper. Pensionable-age exemption is **{{basicExemptionPensionAge|money}}/month** instead, not an additional allowance. An exemption reduces taxable income; it is not a cash benefit |
| Capital gains | **{{incomeTax|pct}}** on taxable gains | Not on all sale proceeds. Cost basis, eligible losses and exemptions matter; an eligible [investment account](investment-account.html) can defer tax under its own rules |
| Dividends from Estonian companies | Usually **no further personal income tax** | Standard company-level distribution tax applies. Redistribution of historic 14/86-taxed dividends can still require **7% withholding**; see the [company guide](company.html) |
| Foreign dividends | Exempt in Estonia **if the underlying profit or dividend was taxed abroad** | Exempt dividends still go in **table 8.8**. Under ordinary taxation, untaxed foreign dividends go in **table 8.1** and are taxable. Investment-account treatment has additional rules; keep evidence |
| Qualifying residential rent | **17.6% of gross rent before personal reliefs** | {{incomeTax|pct}} on 80% after the automatic 20% deduction. This is not after-cost investment profit. Short-term accommodation and subletting do not qualify; see [rental and property tax](real-estate.html) |
| Company retained profits | Generally **not taxed merely when earned** | Not a blanket exemption for company spending: distributions, fringe benefits, gifts and non-business expenses have their own rules |
| Company profit distributions | Standard **22/78 of the net dividend** | Equivalent to 22% of the total dividend-plus-tax budget, not 22% of the amount the owner receives. Exempt redistributions and equity repayments need separate analysis |
| Social tax | **{{socialTax|pct}}** | Employment and board remuneration rules apply; genuine dividends are not salary. The 2026 minimum-obligation base is **{{socialTaxMinimumBaseMonthly|money}}/month** (€292.38 tax). Minimum-payment exceptions and health-insurance eligibility are not inferred from this table |
| Asset ownership | Check asset-specific taxes | A portfolio balance is not the same as a tax bill, but land and motor-vehicle taxes must not be omitted from spending simply because a broad wealth-tax label says “none” |
| Inheritances / gifts | Receipt and later disposal are different events | Inherited property is not taxed on receipt in Estonia; later gains can be taxable. Gifted property, company/employer gifts, foreign taxes and inherited obligations need separate checks |
| Standard VAT | **{{vat|pct}}** | From 1 July 2025; reduced rates and exempt supplies also exist |

## Filing year is not the income year

For **2025 income declared in 2026**, the filing deadline was **30 April 2026**.
The additional-payment and final refund deadline is **1 October 2026**.
Electronic refunds started **5 March 2026** where no further verification was
needed. Foreign income or capital gains do not by themselves establish a separate
October-only refund schedule. Check your own decision and outstanding requests.

These dates do **not** make the 2026 €700 exemption applicable to 2025 income.
Use the rules for the income year and confirm the relevant filing calendar.
Sources: [EMTA foreign-income declaration deadline](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/taxable-income/income-derived-foreign-state)
and [2026 refund/payment timetable](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/declaration-income/refund-and-additional-payment-income-tax).

## What this means for a FIRE plan

Compare spendable, after-tax cash flows, not headline rates alone. Tax deferral
is not tax exemption. Keep supporting records, distinguish personal assets from
company assets, and budget for [health insurance](health-insurance.html) as well
as property, investment and household costs.

**Still case-specific:** residency and treaties, evidence of foreign tax, eligible
assets/losses, gifts and inheritances, payroll exceptions and final tax/refund
amounts. The calculator is not a tax-filing engine and does not automatically
model every tax in this overview. See the [source register](sources.html) for
the limited review scope.
