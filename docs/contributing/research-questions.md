# Research questions for contributors

These are unresolved review prompts, not verified claims or product recommendations.
They preserve the topics from the former public research-prompt collection without
carrying forward its unsupported research-round completion labels.

For each answer, inspect the primary source itself. Record its URL, section,
effective date/applicable period, date checked, scope and affected implementation
in `docs/guide/verification.md`. Distinguish proposals from enacted rules. A search
summary or AI answer is not evidence. Do not include personal plans or account data.

## Pension, income and healthcare

1. What are the employee/state Pillar II contributions and change deadlines?
2. What income supports a Pillar III deduction, in what order, subject to what caps and tax-paid limits? Can any unused deduction transfer between spouses?
3. Which acquisition-history, age and duration conditions govern Pillar II/III payouts, taxes, early exits and rejoining? How are fund payments recalculated?
4. Which future cohort retirement ages are official rather than estimated? How does qualifying service differ from pension units?
5. Which salary, board-fee, state-paid and special-period contributions establish pension service and healthcare? Which minimum-payment exceptions apply?
6. Which spouse/child routes and voluntary-insurance conditions establish coverage, with what dates and premiums?
7. Which income-tax exemptions, payroll rules and family benefits apply in the relevant year? Which announced reforms were subsequently changed?

## Investments and ownership

8. Which account institutions and assets qualify for investment-account treatment? How do foreign banks and partner custodians affect this?
9. How are multiple same-owner accounts, transfers, currency conversions, already-taxed receipts and reporting handled?
10. How do crypto acquisition date and provider licensing affect gains, losses and wrapper eligibility? What about swaps, staking and mining?
11. How are foreign dividends, withholding, declaration deadlines and credits handled?
12. How should OÜ contributed capital, share premium, retained profits and extraction taxes be reconciled without taxing principal as profit?
13. Can gifts create the recipient's contribution allowance, and what documentation and ownership conditions apply?
14. What changes on emigration under domestic law and the actual destination treaty? Is Estonian tax deferral recognised there?
15. Which law governs a cross-border couple's marital property, divorce and inheritance? What contractual/notarial evidence is required?

## Housing, protection and commercial snapshots

16. What lending limits, affordability stress tests and housing guarantees apply, and which are bank-specific rather than legal limits?
17. What taxes and exemptions apply to rent, home sales, land and mortgage interest?
18. What early-repayment rights, fees and term/payment options follow from the law and the actual loan contract?
19. What do deposit guarantees, temporary-high-balance rules and investor compensation actually cover? How do joint ownership and asset segregation differ?
20. What are the current fund fees, index, domicile, replication, holdings and size for any named ETF? Record the dated factsheet, not an undated comparison.
21. What do LHV, Swedbank, Tuleva and any named alternative currently offer and charge? Verify exchange/ISIN availability and reporting with the actual provider; do not assume a partner service has the bank's legal status.

After any finding, identify all affected prose, constants, examples and tests;
update them together and rebuild the site. Leave provider availability or personal
eligibility explicitly unverified when public evidence is insufficient.
